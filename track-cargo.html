DELIVERY JOURNEY

Your Cargo's Journey

Follow the different stages a shipment may pass through before reaching its destination.

1

Shipment Registered

The cargo company records the shipment details and creates a tracking number.

2

Cargo Picked Up

The cargo is collected and prepared for transportation.

3

In Transit

The shipment is travelling towards its destination.

4

Out for Delivery

The shipment is on its final delivery journey, where applicable.

5

Delivered

The cargo has reached its destination and delivery has been confirmed.

How Cargo Tracking Works

01
